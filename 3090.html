Everything you need to know about egg · 2027 …

… you will find on this website.

egg is a meeting for highly gifted people. Historically, it is a European offshoot of the ggg999 meeting, which was founded as a meeting for members of the Triple Nine Society (TNS) back in 2009.
After the success of ggg999, 2011 saw the first egg (then called egg999) in London.

Highly gifted is not a well-defined term, but then 3SD above average might equally not be something everyone knows about. So if you really want to know, start here.

We provide a lot of information to future participants in the PDFs below, most of this information has been updated from the year before. Please read through all of it and try to not send us emails with questions, unless necessary.
